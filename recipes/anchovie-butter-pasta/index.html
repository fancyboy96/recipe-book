Anchovie Butter Pasta

Pantry pasta with anchovies, butter, and parmesan.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Salt
  • Butter
  • Anchovies
  • Olive Oil
  • Grana Padano (or parmesan)

Directions

  1. 1 Boil pasta with salty water
  2. 2 Heat pan and lightly fry one can of anchovies (broken up)
  3. 3 Add butter and melt
  4. 4 Add pasta water and simmer
  5. 5 Add grana padano and melt
  6. 6 Add olive oil
  7. 7 When cooked, add pasta, combine, and serve
