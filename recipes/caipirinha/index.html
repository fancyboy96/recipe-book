Caipirinha (Non-alcoholic)

A refreshing lime and agave cocktail.

Ingredients

  • 2 limes
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 tablespoons agave syrup
  • ½ can of condensed milk
  • 2 cups ice

Directions

  1. 1 Cut limes into eighths
  2. 2 Add limes (rind and all) to blender with water and agave and pulse 9 times
  3. 3 Strain juice
  4. 4 Add juice back into blender with condensed milk and ice, and blend
