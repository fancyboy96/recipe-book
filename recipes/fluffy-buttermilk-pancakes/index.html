Fluffy Buttermilk Pancakes

Sunday-morning pancakes that are impossibly fluffy.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 3 tbsp melted butter

Directions

  1. 1 Whisk the dry ingredients together in a large bowl.
  2. 2 Whisk the wet ingredients in a separate bowl.
  3. 3 Combine the two, stirring until just mixed. Let the batter rest for 5 minutes.
  4. 4 Cook on a buttered griddle over medium heat, about 2–3 minutes per side.
