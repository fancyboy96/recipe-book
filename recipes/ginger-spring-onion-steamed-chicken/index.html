Ginger & Spring Onion Steamed Chicken

Steamed chicken with ginger-spring onion sauce.

Ingredients

  • Chicken pieces with skin on or off
  • Rice
  • 2 knobs of ginger
  • 6 spring onions
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp sambal
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 4 tbsp neutral oil

Directions

  1. 1 Prepare plate with small bowl turned upside down in center of plate
  2. 2 Assemble chicken pieces, ginger, spring onions on plate
  3. 3 Assemble large pot with about 2cm water, and place plate on steaming plate
  4. 4 Steam for 30 minutes
  5. 5 The upside down bowl will capture a chicken broth from the steamed chicken - save this
  6. 6 Prepare sauce: Add finely chopped spring onions and minced ginger to bowl
  7. 7 Heat neutral oil on pan until hot (using the 'chopstick test'), and pour over spring onions and minced ginger to infuse
  8. 8 Mix sambal, oyster sauce, soy sauce, chicken broth into the ginger and spring onion infused oil
  9. 9 Serve chicken and sauce over rice
