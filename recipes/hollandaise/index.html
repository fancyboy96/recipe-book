Hollandaise

Classic hollandaise sauce.

Ingredients

  • 3 egg yolks
  • ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper (paprika and chilli powder is a good substitute)
  • ¼ teaspoon of salt
  • 1 ½ tablespoons of lemon juice
  • 1 ½ tablespoons of water
  • 175 grams of salted butter

Directions

  1. 1 Melt butter
  2. 2 Use stick blender to combine egg yolks, cayenne pepper, salt, lemon juice, and water
  3. 3 With stick blender on high, slowly pour in the butter in a thin stream into the eggs (over approximately 45 seconds)
  4. 4 Blitz for a further 10 seconds while moving stick blender up and down, if too thick, then add warm water one teaspoon at a time
  5. 5 Can refrigerate and reheat in warm water bath
