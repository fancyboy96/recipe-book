Microwave Sticky Date Pudding

Sticky date pudding made in the microwave.

Ingredients

  • Butter
  • Brown sugar
  • Cream
  • Dates
  • Baking soda
  • Flour
  • Cinnamon
  • Salt

Directions

  1. 1 Add 1 tablespoon of butter, 2 tablespoons of brown sugar, 1 tablespoon of cream, and a pinch of salt and microwave for 30 second intervals until melted and mix to combine (Sauce)
  2. 2 In mugs / small bowls, add two dates, one tablespoon of butter, 2 tablespoons of cream, and ¼ teaspoons of baking soda. Combine and microwave for 60 seconds
  3. 3 Mashe into paste then add ¼ teaspoons of flour, ⅛ teaspoons of cinnamon, a pinch of salt, and 1 tablespoon of brown sugar and microwave for 60 seconds
  4. 4 Pour sauce over pudding and serve
